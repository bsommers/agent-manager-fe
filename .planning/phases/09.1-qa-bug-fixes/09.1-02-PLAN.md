<objective>
Fix QA BUG 2 (QA-02): the destructive safety modal is `fixed ... z-50` but floating panes carry an inline `z-index` of 100 or more, so the panes are painted over the modal and its Cancel button cannot be clicked (Escape still works). The same defect is latent everywhere: AddAgentModal, GlobalSearchModal, CommandPalette and ShortcutHelp use `z-[500]`, which a pane overtakes after about 400 raises (`bringToFront` computes `max(zIndex) + 1` with no bound and runs on every pane mouse-down, resize start, toast click, palette "Go to" and search-hit jump), and the toast stack is `z-50`, always below the panes.

Fix, for all modals and overlays at once: (1) one z-index scale in one module (`src/styles/zLayers.ts`): panes live in [100, 8999], overlays at 9000, destructive confirmations at 9100, toasts at 9200; (2) every overlay and the toast stack is rendered through one `OverlayPortal` into `document.body` with a zero-size fixed wrapper that carries the layer's inline z-index, so a future ancestor that creates a stacking context (transform, filter, backdrop-blur, z-index) cannot trap an overlay below the pane layer; (3) pane z-index is bounded at the source: `bringToFront` re-ranks panes into the contiguous range starting at 100 (an unbounded counter is replaced by a rank), and `FloatingPane` clamps the stored value to the ceiling at render, so externally supplied values (later persisted layouts) cannot exceed the overlays either. Class-based z utilities are removed from overlay files because Tailwind cannot read the shared constants and a second source of truth would drift.

Why this and not "raise the number": a bigger constant only moves the failure point and leaves the root cause (unbounded counter, overlays inside whatever stacking context their parent happens to have). Why a rank and not a clamp in the store: with a plain cap, the raised pane and the others would tie at the cap and DOM order would decide, so the raised pane could stay behind.

Purpose: Cancel and Confirm on the safety modal always work, and no overlay can ever be covered by a pane.
Output: `zLayers.ts`, `OverlayPortal.tsx`, edits to six overlay components, `FloatingPane.tsx` and `agentStore.ts` (bringToFront only), one new test file, ADR-009.
